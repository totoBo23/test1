# Brand Style Standard

This brand system is designed for a rent-overpayment legal service.
Tone: serious, trustworthy, human, and easy to use.

## 1) Brand personality

- Serious: legal clarity, structured layouts, high readability.
- Human: warm neutrals, people-centered visuals, plain language.
- Modern: clean cards, strong spacing rhythm, mobile-first behavior.
- Action-oriented: clear CTA hierarchy and simple form flow.

## 2) Typography (open source)

- Heading font: `Merriweather` (SIL Open Font License)
- Body/UI font: `Source Sans 3` (SIL Open Font License)

Use:
- H1/H2/major headings: `Merriweather`
- Body text/forms/buttons/navigation: `Source Sans 3`

## 3) Core colors

- Background: `#F7F3EC`
- Surface: `#FFFDFA`
- Surface Alt: `#F6EFE6`
- Primary (trust blue): `#154F78`
- Primary strong: `#0F3B5A`
- Accent (warm support): `#B86435`
- Text: `#1F2B35`
- Muted text: `#556271`
- Border: `#DCCFBE`
- Success: `#2B6B53`
- Danger: `#B13A3F`

## 4) Component rules

- Buttons:
  - Primary: blue background, white text.
  - Secondary: white background, blue border/text.
  - Ghost: neutral border, dark text.
- Cards:
  - Rounded corners (`14px`), subtle border, soft shadow.
- Inputs:
  - Minimum height `45px`, clear focus ring, high contrast text.
- Pills/badges:
  - Use for trust highlights and short proof points.

## 5) Visual/imagery direction

- Prefer real images or illustrations showing people (faces, consultations, support moments).
- Avoid generic corporate stock style.
- Keep warm skin-tone/neutral environments where possible.
- Use supportive caption text, not sales-heavy phrasing.

## 6) Accessibility baseline

- Strong color contrast for text and controls.
- Visible focus outlines for keyboard users.
- Mobile-first: single-column forms and cards on small screens.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
